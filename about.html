About CPB

CPB stands for "Compress PDF to 100KB". We built it for a simple, common problem: you have a PDF that is too big, and the website you are uploading it to says the limit is 100 KB, 200 KB or 500 KB.

What makes it different

  • You choose the size. Pick a preset or type your own target in KB.
  • Deleted right after. Your PDF is sent over an encrypted connection, compressed, and removed from our server as soon as your copy is ready.
  • Free and simple. No account, no watermark, no limit on how many files you try.
  • Works on phones. The page is made to be easy to use on a small screen.

Good to know

CPB shrinks the pictures inside your PDF and keeps the text, so text usually stays selectable. For very small sizes, and only if you allow it, pages can be saved as images, which makes the text unselectable. Keep your original for editing and copying, and use the compressed copy for uploading.
